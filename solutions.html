INTELLIGENCE MEETS INFRASTRUCTURE

OUR SOLUTIONS

InfraNova combines camera-based monitoring, AI image analysis, and connected communication to support safer infrastructure.

🕳️

Pothole Detection

AI image analysis can identify visible potholes and help create reports for inspection and repair.

🚧

Manhole Detection

A trained vision model can flag visible open or uncovered manholes for further verification.

⛰️

Landslide Monitoring

Suitable sensors can monitor conditions such as soil moisture and slope movement. Threshold-based warnings can support early investigation.

🚑

Accident Alerts

A suitable AI model can flag possible road accidents and send an alert for human review.

📷

Smart Camera Network

ESP32-CAM units can capture images at selected intervals and provide visual information for infrastructure monitoring.

🖥️

Monitoring Dashboard

A dashboard can organise alerts, image records, hazard categories, and available location data for authorised personnel.

OUR APPROACH

Detect → Analyse → Communicate → Verify → Respond. InfraNova is designed to support human decision-making. AI detections should be verified before emergency action or infrastructure maintenance decisions.
