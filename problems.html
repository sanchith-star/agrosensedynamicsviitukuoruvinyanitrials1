THE CHALLENGES WE FACE

INFRASTRUCTURE PROBLEMS

Damaged roads, unsafe infrastructure, and delayed hazard reporting can affect public safety. InfraNova explores how connected monitoring could help address these challenges.

🕳️

01. Potholes

Potholes can damage vehicles, disrupt traffic, and create risks for drivers and two-wheeler riders. Delayed identification can make road maintenance more difficult.

⚠️

02. Open Manholes

Missing or uncovered manhole covers can create serious hazards for pedestrians, cyclists, and road users, especially in poor visibility.

⛰️

03. Landslide Risks

Unstable slopes, changing soil conditions, and heavy rainfall can threaten nearby roads and communities. Monitoring may help identify warning signs.

🚑

04. Road Accidents

Accidents may go unreported for some time, particularly in isolated locations. Delays in communicating an incident can complicate emergency response.

🛣️

05. Road Surface Damage

Cracks and surface deterioration can worsen if they are not identified and maintained in a timely manner.

📡

06. Delayed Reporting

Infrastructure authorities may depend on public complaints or manual inspections. This can make it difficult to identify and prioritise every hazard quickly.

THE CORE PROBLEM

Many infrastructure hazards need timely identification, accurate location information, and effective communication. InfraNova proposes a camera-based AI monitoring system to support these activities.
